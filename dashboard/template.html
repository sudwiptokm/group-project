Signal control results: RL vs classical controllers

Every comparison from the project in one place: isolated intersection, 3-signal corridor, geometry sweeps, the safety weight λ, MAPPO vs IPPO, and per-vehicle-type delay. Delay is seconds of time loss per completed trip (lower is better) unless marked. Error bars and bands show ±1 SD across seeds. Every chart has a table view.

Corridor: who wins depends on the geometry

On evenly spaced signals, the fixed offset plan (green wave) beats every learned and reactive controller. On irregular spacing it loses to independent DQN. Classical controllers and IDQN use n=10 seeds on regular/irregular. Variants 2 and 3 use n=5 for classical controllers and n=3 for IDQN.

Geometry dose-response

r = length of the first block ÷ total span, with the span held fixed. Shaded bands mark ratios where IDQN has lower delay than the green wave. The reversal happens in two bounded bands, not as a monotonic trend. Green wave and max-pressure use n=5, IDQN n=3 (zero-shot, no retraining).

Safety weight λ: a regulariser, not a trade-off

IDQN retrained at each λ (n=3). Delay bottoms out at λ=0.25, below both λ=0 and the project default λ=0.5, while the safety penalty per trip keeps falling as λ grows. The dashed line is the irregular geometry.

Centralised critic (MAPPO) vs independent (IPPO)

Single-seed smoke runs. MAPPO does not beat IPPO on either geometry, including after one round of retuning (lower learning rate, batch 64, 512-unit critic).

Delay by vehicle type

The learned controller spreads delay unevenly: motorcycles fare worst and cars best. Both non-learning baselines stay close to type-uniform. The efficiency term of the reward is not weighted by vehicle type; only the safety term is.

Lane-blocking incident

Corridor peak with and without an incident (n=3, seeds 42–44). Delay is measured inside the incident window, where the effect is concentrated.

Isolated intersection

Stage 1: four single-agent algorithms vs a fixed-time plan (λ=0.5). A well-timed static plan beats every RL agent. The cause is structural: with too short a minimum green, amber phases eat the capacity. The timing sweeps below show that.

Paired statistics

Paired differences across matched seeds, from analysis/headline_stats.csv. The p-values come from exact sign-flip tests. With n=3, the smallest possible p is 0.25, so those rows are descriptive only.
